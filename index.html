    Randomize shuffles the gaps between the clips inside the covered width; every press gives a new, less regular set of folds. You can also drag any clip in the view: pulling one drags its neighbours along once the fabric between them goes taut.

    03Cloth

    Weight
    Drape

    Raise the stiffness or the thickness and the hem stops following the pleats: it keeps a few broad waves, pushes the bottom of the curtain out wider, and lies on the floor as a band instead of crumpling. Thickness counts steeply, as it does in real cloth. Blackout and velvet presets start with a thick, firm hem. If you soften the hem afterwards, spread the curtain flat and gather it again to get the pleats back.

    Bringing it into 3ds Max
    1. If the download is a .zip, unzip it. The .obj and .mtl stay together.
    2. File › Import › Import and pick the .obj.
    3. In the OBJ dialog choose Import as single mesh, keep Flip ZY-axis ticked, and set the model units to cm.
    4. The pivot is on the floor under the middle of the rod, so the curtain drops straight onto your floor.
    5. The mesh is all quads: add TurboSmooth for close-ups. For sheers, export without thickness and use VRay2SidedMtl.
